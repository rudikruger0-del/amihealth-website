How AMI Works

AMI is designed to reduce cognitive load in clinical environments — surfacing what matters, when it matters, without replacing clinical judgement.

1. Secure clinical data intake

AMI ingests laboratory data including CBCs, chemistry panels, and reports — whether digitally generated or scanned. Data is handled within strict access boundaries and audit controls.

2. Deterministic extraction — no guesswork

Values are extracted exactly as reported using controlled OCR and rule-based parsing. When data is missing or ambiguous, uncertainty is flagged instead of inferred.

No hallucinated values. Ever.

3. Canonical clinical normalization

Results are mapped into a unified internal schema, allowing consistent interpretation across different laboratories, formats, and reference ranges.

This removes formatting noise and standardises clinical context.

4. Pattern-focused clinical reasoning

AMI evaluates combinations of results to highlight clinically relevant patterns — such as inflammatory dominance, cytopenias, renal stress, or discordant markers.

AMI does not diagnose. It highlights what deserves attention.

5. Explainable outputs for rapid review

Every alert and summary is traceable to the values and trends that triggered it. Outputs are designed to support verification, not blind trust.

Built for clinicians. Designed for real-world pressure.
