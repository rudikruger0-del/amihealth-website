How AMI Works

Clinical intelligence designed to support — not replace — medical judgement.

1. Secure clinical data intake

AMI ingests laboratory reports, imaging findings, and structured clinical data. Scanned and digital reports are handled identically. Each case is processed in isolation.

2. Deterministic extraction — not guesswork

OCR and parsers extract values exactly as reported. Units, reference ranges, and flags are preserved. Missing data is explicitly flagged — never fabricated.

3. Canonical clinical normalization

Values are mapped into a standardized clinical schema to ensure consistency across laboratories and report formats.

4. Pattern recognition — not diagnosis

AMI highlights clinically relevant patterns and risk combinations. Final interpretation and decisions always remain with the clinician.
