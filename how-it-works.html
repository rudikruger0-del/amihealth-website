How AMI Works

Clinical intelligence designed to support — not replace — medical judgement.

  1. Secure clinical data intake
    CBCs, chemistry, imaging and reports are ingested securely — scanned or digital.

  2. Deterministic extraction — not guesswork
    OCR and parsers extract values exactly as reported. No fabrication. No assumptions.

  3. Canonical clinical normalization
    Results are mapped into a consistent schema for reliable interpretation.

  4. Pattern recognition — not diagnosis
    AMI highlights clinically relevant patterns. Final decisions remain with the doctor.
