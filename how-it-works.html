How AMI Works

Clinical intelligence designed to support — not replace — medical judgement.

1. Secure clinical data intake

AMI ingests laboratory reports, imaging findings, and structured clinical data. Scanned and digital reports are handled identically.

2. Deterministic extraction — not guesswork

OCR and parsers extract values exactly as reported. Units, ranges, and flags are preserved. Missing data is never fabricated.

3. Canonical clinical normalization

Values are mapped into a consistent clinical schema so interpretation is independent of lab or report format.

4. Pattern recognition — not diagnosis

AMI highlights clinically relevant patterns and risks. Final interpretation always remains with the clinician.
