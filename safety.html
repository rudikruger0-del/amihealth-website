Safety First. Always.

AMI is built to support clinicians — not replace them.

AMI is clinical support software

AMI provides structured interpretation and pattern recognition to assist clinicians in reviewing clinical data more efficiently.

AMI does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend admission, or make treatment decisions.

Clinical responsibility remains with the doctor

All outputs generated by AMI are intended to support — not override — clinical judgement.

The attending clinician remains fully responsible for:

  • Clinical assessment and diagnosis
  • Treatment decisions
  • Patient communication
  • Final interpretation of all results

No hallucination by design

AMI is deliberately engineered to avoid false certainty.

  • No value is invented if missing or unclear
  • Parsed values preserve original units and ranges
  • Ambiguity is flagged rather than assumed

If data cannot be confidently extracted, AMI will state this explicitly.

Explainability over black-box output

Every flagged abnormality is accompanied by:

  • The physiological rationale
  • Relevant clinical context
  • Typical verification steps clinicians consider

AMI is designed to think *like a cautious clinician*, not a chatbot.

Severity & urgency are conservative

Severity classifications (normal → critical) are intentionally conservative.

When multiple serious abnormalities coexist, AMI escalates attention — but does not dictate action.

Designed for medico-legal defensibility

AMI’s language, structure, and output tone are intentionally restrained.

  • No absolute claims
  • No diagnostic labels presented as fact
  • No instruction to treat or admit

This approach aligns with real-world medico-legal expectations.

Data privacy & isolation

Each report is processed independently. No patient data is reused across cases.

AMI does not learn from individual patient data.

Built with real clinical workflows in mind

AMI was designed for:

  • Busy emergency departments
  • After-hours general practice
  • High-volume laboratory review

It exists to reduce cognitive overload — not to replace professional judgement.

Technology that respects clinical responsibility

Built in South Africa. Designed for South African clinicians.
