Clinical Use Cases

AMI is designed for real clinical environments — where time is limited, interruptions are constant, and decisions matter.

Emergency Departments

Supports rapid prioritisation of abnormal CBCs and chemistry results. Highlights patterns that may warrant closer review while clinicians manage multiple competing demands.

After-hours & On-call Cover

Provides structured summaries when full clinical context is unavailable. Particularly useful during fatigue, handovers, and remote consultations.

General Practice

Assists with early identification of inflammatory, renal, haematological, or metabolic stress across routine and follow-up investigations.

Specialist Review

Presents complex laboratory data in a consistent, structured format for oncology, nephrology, internal medicine, and multidisciplinary review.
