What happens after upload?

        
              
  1. Your file is stored securely in the private reports bucket.
    2.           
  2. A signed URL is generated and sent to the AMI Blood AI engine on Hugging Face.
    3.           
  3. The engine extracts CBC values, applies AMI rule logic and generates an interpretation.
    4.           
  4. The AI result is saved back into the reports table.
    5.           
  5. On the dashboard, you can open the original PDF and view the AI JSON result.
    6.         
        

          Note: AMI is a clinical decision support tool and does not replace clinician judgement.
